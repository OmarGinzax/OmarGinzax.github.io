# AI Rules & Project Goals — Omar Ginza Portfolio

## 1. Project Goal
Build and maintain a clean, static GitHub Pages portfolio website (`index.html` + assets) for **Omar Ginza**.
The site must establish a sharp, credible career profile across three core pillars:
1. **Game Developer** — Shipping playable experiences (e.g., *Altalis* on Steam, *Heart String* on itch.io), systems design, and world/narrative structuring.
2. **Programmer** — Writing modular, production-ready C# and Unity architectures (object manipulation, progression gating, gameplay systems).
3. **Project Manager** — Leading multidisciplinary teams, planning milestones, coordinating across art/code/design/writing, and shipping builds on schedule.

---

## 2. Anti-"AI-Slop" Styling & Design Rules
To keep the site looking hand-crafted, intentional, and genuinely designed by a developer rather than generated by a template:

### Visual & Layout Restraint
- **No generic AI tropes:** Avoid purple-to-blue neon gradients, glowing glassmorphism cards, floating 3D blobs, particle backgrounds, or excessive box-shadows.
- **Editorial & Industrial Grid:** Use crisp 1px structural borders (`--line`), asymmetric two-column splits, strong typographic hierarchy, and generous negative space.
- **Tactile Color Palette:** Stick to a grounded, high-contrast dark theme (deep slate/charcoal `#0C1119`, warm off-white text `#ECE8E0`, muted steel `#9199A9`, and a single warm industrial amber/brass accent `#E0A458`).
- **Typography with Character:** Pair a bold, condensed industrial display face (`Big Shoulders Display`) for headers/numbers with a clean, technical geometric grotesque (`Hanken Grotesk`) or monospace accents for metadata, dates, and technical specs.
- **One Signature Moment:** Keep motion purposeful. If there is an animation (like a subtle frame reveal or smooth carousel snap), let it feel like a game UI or editorial layout—never animate every element on scroll.

### Copywriting & Content Rules
- **Concrete Proof Over Buzzwords:** Never use filler phrases like *"Passionate visionary leveraging cutting-edge synergies"* or *"Unleashing immersive digital experiences."*
- **Lead with Numbers & Outcomes:** Always state team size, role, engine/tools, deliverable, and platform (e.g., *"Led a 6-person team to ship a public Steam demo"*, *"Structured 4 routes, 14 branching paths, and 14 endings"*).
- **No Emoji Spam:** Do not put rocket ships, sparkles, or random emojis in headings or bullet points. Use clean typographic markers, index numbers (`01`, `02`), or subtle borders instead.
- **Honest Technical Detail:** Describe *what* was programmed or managed (e.g., *"modular ability-gating framework in C#"*, *"milestone tracking across art and code"*).

---

## 3. Structural & Technical Standards (GitHub Pages Ready)
- **Zero-Build Static Architecture:** Plain, semantic HTML5, modern CSS (Custom Properties, Grid, Flexbox, `scroll-snap`), and lightweight vanilla JavaScript. No unnecessary frameworks or heavy npm build steps required to deploy.
- **Fast & Resilient Assets:**
  - All local images and downloadable files (like screenshots and CV PDFs) must match exact case-sensitive paths for GitHub Pages (Linux-hosted) compatibility (e.g., `./Altalis.jpg` vs `assets/altalis.jpg`).
  - Provide clean CSS fallback frames if an image hasn't been added yet.
- **Accessibility & Responsiveness:**
  - Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
  - Maintain clear `:focus-visible` states and semantic landmarks (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`).
  - Ensure layouts collapse cleanly on mobile (`< 760px`) without horizontal overflow.

---

## 4. Content Pillars Checklist
Every major project or experience entry should clearly answer:
1. **What is it & where does it live?** (Title, genre, studio, year, direct link to Steam / itch.io / GitHub).
2. **What did Omar code or design?** (Specific Unity/C# systems, mechanics, or narrative architecture).
3. **How did Omar lead or manage it?** (Team headcount, milestone planning, cross-discipline coordination).
