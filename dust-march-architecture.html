Technical Architecture & Systems Documentation

Dust March — Roguelike & Persistence

Author: Omar Ginza (Game Designer, PM & Programmer) Genre: 2D Roguelike Real-Time Strategy Engine: Unity 2D URP / C# Platform: Windows (itch.io)

Dust March combines a node-based roguelike overland campaign with real-time tactical squad battles. Because Unity unloads scene objects between the strategic map (MapScene) and combat arenas (Easy/Medium/Hard/FinalBattle), I designed a two-tier persistence bridge paired with a fatigue, combat trauma, and permadeath loop that forces players to rotate and draft squads strategically.

System 01 · Cross-Scene Persistence Architecture

Two-Tier Data Bridge & Transactional Map Rollback

To avoid tightly coupling the strategic overland scene to the tactical combat scenes, persistence is split into long-term JSON disk storage and a transient static memory bus (SquadTransferData) that ferries live squad states across scene loads.

JSON Disk Serialization SaveManager.cs

Wraps top-level unit and node lists for JsonUtility serialization into inventory.json, mapstate.json, and squad.json inside Application.persistentDataPath.

Transactional Defeat Rollback mapstate_backup.json

Before loading a battle scene, ConfirmTravel() snapshots the map graph. If the player loses the battle, the map rolls back to the pre-mission node—while keeping all unit injuries and permadeaths permanent.

Transient Inter-Scene Bus SquadTransferData.cs

Lightweight static C# data pipe holding activeSquad, removedCharacters, playerWon, and pendingGoldReward across scene transitions without heavyweight scene dependencies.

Persistent Roster Singleton InventoryManager.cs

DontDestroyOnLoad manager that reconciles removedCharacters after battle, tracks healthy vs. wounded unit counts, and triggers campaign Game Over if zero deployable units remain.

System 02 · Roguelike Roster Attrition

Fatigue, Combat Trauma & Permadeath Loop

In many strategy games, players can steamroll the campaign with a single over-leveled squad. To prevent this, I designed an Availability & Combat Trauma Engine that penalizes reckless wins and forces roster rotation.

Unit Condition After Battle Availability Penalty Recovery Requirement Engine Behavior
Deployed (Overland Jump) -1 Availability 1 jump benched in reserve (+1/hop) Units dropping to ≤ 0 are automatically evicted from active squad slots.
50% – 75% HP Remaining availability = -1 (Moderate Trauma) 2 overland jumps in reserve Applied post-battle by UnitStats.ApplyAvailabilityPenalty().
25% – 50% HP Remaining availability = -2 (Severe Trauma) 3 overland jumps in reserve Unit is locked out of deployment until availability returns to ≥ 1.
≤ 25% HP Remaining availability = -3 (Critical Trauma) 4 overland jumps in reserve Forces player to recruit or field backup squads from the procedural market.
0 HP (Killed in Action) Permadeath (isDead = true) Permanent removal Displayed in red on BattleSquadStatusPanel, then scrubbed from inventory.json.
System 03 · Procedural Campaign & Unit Drafting

DAG Map Progression & Genetic Recruit Generation

Every overland jump advances the player along a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) of mission nodes while refreshing a procedural mercenary market with randomized archetypes, genetic traits, and modular paper-doll sprites.

Forward-Pruning DAG Map MapManager.cs & AreaNode.cs

Locks previously visited nodes (LockAllAreasExcept) and unlocks only forward adjacent edges. Procedural nodes scale enemy wave density (1–2 on Easy up to 8 on Final) and gold payouts (100g–1000g).

Procedural Unit & Trait Drafting ShopManager.cs

Generates 4 recruits per jump across 4 classes (Marksman, Rifleman, Frontliner, Rusher) and rolls trait mutations: Strong (+20% ATK), Valiant (+15% HP), Eagle Eyed (+10% Range), or Positive (10% faster fire rate).

Modular Paper-Doll Assembly CharacterBuilder.cs

Decouples unit data from visuals by binding rolled cosmetic indices (hair, head, body, arms, legs, weapon) to Unity 2D Animation SpriteResolver categories at runtime.

Exponential Level-Up Curve & NG+ Loop GameOverManager.cs

Awards 200 EXP on win (50 EXP on loss), granting +1 ATK and +3 HP per level while doubling next-level EXP requirements. Clearing the Final node triggers a New Game+ map reset while preserving your veteran roster.

System 04 · Real-Time Tactical Combat

Dynamic Grid Formations & Line-of-Sight AI

Inside combat scenes, players command squads using mouse marquee boxes or touch gestures against escalating enemy waves powered by A* pathfinding.

Centered Formation Math & Anti-Crowding GameController.cs

When ordering N selected units to a target point, computes a centered ⌈√N⌉ grid formation with 0.5m spacing and [-0.2, 0.2] positional jitter so units never deadlock on the same A* pathfinding node.

Aggressive vs. Passive Line-of-Sight AI UnitController.cs & UnitShooting.cs

In Passive mode, units hold ground and fire in range. In Aggressive mode, units cast 2D raycasts toward targets and automatically command their AIPath component to flank around obstacles blocking line of sight.
